# Proofspar

Naming and product-page decision, 3 November 2026.

The owner asked for a unique, powerful, appropriate name for the new accuracy pen test and instructed continuation. The chosen name is **Proofspar by ThirdOcular**. The line is **Put your AI's answers under pressure.** The stable product URL remains `/accuracy/` as specified in the brief.

## Why this name

Proof points to evidence a reviewer can inspect. Spar points to controlled challenge: difficult questions, changed facts and customer pushback. Together they describe the intended work without claiming a perfect answer or an industry credential. The product uses ThirdOcular's existing iris, colors and typefaces.

An initial exact-name web search on 3 November found no obvious same-name AI evaluation product. It returned an unrelated retail text match for the concatenated words. Searches for adjacent candidates found existing [Factward](https://www.factward.com/) and [Proofward](https://gitlab.com/explore/projects/topics/verification?archived=true&sort=created_desc), so those were rejected. This was a preliminary collision check, not proof of worldwide exclusivity or a trademark clearance. No domain was bought and no registration claim is made.

## What exists

- A complete product-page preview at `/accuracy/`, with an honest development status, intended scope, planned process, report contents, first-pilot contact and limitations.
- A prominent homepage section and a navigation link throughout the maintained site.
- `/lessons/`, `/retired/` and `/about/`, connected to the two published project stories.
- A 1200 by 630 JPEG sharing image, with editable HTML source in `scripts/proofspar-og.html`.

The testing engine, fictional-agent demonstration, measured checker error and sample evidence report do not exist yet. There are no Proofspar results, customers or completed pilots to advertise. The page does not contain a sample-report section or a simulated test presented as an actual run.

## Changes from the supplied copy

- Kept the approved first-person homepage instead of replacing it with a company sales headline. Proofspar is visible in the hero link, a dedicated section immediately below the hero, and the primary navigation.
- Marked the entire test process as planned. Existing AisleLens code does not establish a working policy-answer checker.
- Used the repository-supported conclusion that Privi's prediction claim was not demonstrated. The new lessons page does not claim a measured disproof.
- Explained the static-versus-runtime distinction without the brief's disputed 19/22 split. The published rubric uses 20/21.
- Omitted unverified market, funding and third-party adoption claims. The three tests for the next direction are questions to validate with buyers, not proven business advantages.
- The seven legacy research HTML pages on the main domain now carry an archive banner and follow the wording rules, as the owner previously requested for old text. Numeric sequences and underlying structured data are unchanged. Two byte-identical JSON aliases give display links compliant names; their original URLs remain available. Original HTML copies are kept in ignored local review storage.
