01 What Labs is
i

Practice on ourselves

ThirdOcular builds and evaluates agentic systems. Labs is where the same discipline gets applied to problems we have personally, on data we personally own, with no customer to reassure and no deadline to protect. We measure before we trust.

ii

One discipline, harder stakes

The products the company sells apply a single method: write down what a thing has to be able to prove, run the check, publish the result including the parts that failed. Labs applies that method where the subject is a life rather than a product page, and where the honest answer is often that a component was measured and then deleted.

iii

Nothing here is finished

Each experiment ships as a long-running build with a changelog, an evidence ledger, and a plain account of what does not work yet. If a claim on a Labs page has no number behind it, it is marked as direction rather than dressed up as a result.

02 Flagship experiment

LifeOS

Local AI · Entity resolution · Temporal reasoning
v0.x · in progress

A long-horizon attempt to turn one family's scattered digital exhaust into a single, evidence-backed, locally computed model of a life. Photographs, documents, mailboxes and file dumps are treated as evidence. The product is the model above them: people, places, events, obligations, and the things that should exist but do not.
