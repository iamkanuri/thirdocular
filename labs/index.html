01 What Labs is
i

Start with curiosity

A question, a personal problem, or an idea worth trying. Labs is where I follow it far enough to learn something.

ii

Show what happened

What worked, what failed, and what remains unfinished. The record matters more than making every experiment sound like a success.

iii

Leave room to stop

Not every project needs to become a business. Retired experiments stay here for their ideas and lessons.

02 Personal archive · build notes

LifeOS

Local AI · Entity resolution · Temporal reasoning
v0.x · in progress

A long-horizon attempt to turn one family's scattered digital exhaust into a single, evidence-backed, locally computed model of a life. Photographs, documents, mailboxes and file dumps are treated as evidence. The product is the model above them: people, places, events, obligations, and the things that should exist but do not.
