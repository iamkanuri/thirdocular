01 · The studio

ThirdOcular builds software and original experiences around the things that deserve a closer look: how we make decisions, how we keep what matters, and how we make room for imagination.

The third eye in our name is a change of perspective. Look beyond the obvious. Notice what is missing. Turn that insight into something people can use, keep, or enjoy.

Founded and run by Sandeep Kanuri, ThirdOcular LLC develops its own products from early experiments through release. Technology is part of the craft. The reason to build comes first.

02 · Products

Our commercial work starts with a specific audience and a problem worth solving. AisleLens explores clearer buying decisions. Pocket Giggles creates small moments of shared imagination.

001 AisleLensLive Buying standards, published and run as tests A buying standard is the set of questions a competent buyer asks in a category, written down, versioned, and executable. AisleLens publishes them, then runs them against a store's real product pages — reporting every requirement as proven, not proven, or requires store access, with the evidence that decided it. 04 · How ideas become products

Observe closely. Make something real. Put it in someone’s hands.

01 / Notice

Start with a real need

Talk to the people who live with a problem. Understand what they do today and what a better experience would change.

02 / Make

Build a focused first version

Develop the software, stories, and design together. Keep the promise small enough to test and useful enough to matter.

03 / Learn

Test in everyday life

Use hands-on sessions, product trials, and feedback to decide what to improve, release, or stop.

04 / Grow

Earn the next step

Develop the ideas people value. Keep room for new products without pretending every experiment deserves a business.
